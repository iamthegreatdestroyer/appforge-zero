## 📊 PROJECT OVERVIEW

### The Mission
Build a working desktop application MVP that:
- Generates Android apps from trending topics (Google Trends + Reddit)
- Morphs templates into new contexts
- Distributes apps to users via Gumroad
- Achieves 500+ users and $5K+/month revenue in 4 weeks

### The Team
- **Size**: 2-3 developers
- **Duration**: 4 weeks (Jan 5-30)
- **Budget**: $0/month
- **Stack**: Electron, React, TypeScript, SQLite

### The Vision
Phase 8A is the foundation for a fully automated app generation and distribution platform. Success here triggers Phase 8B (cloud infrastructure) and Phase 9 (advanced features).

- If user acquisition slow: Reach out to beta testers, social media
